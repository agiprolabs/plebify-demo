How it works

Calibrated, not asserted.

Plebify polls a census-mixed persona swarm, pools their probability judgments, and corrects the result with a calibration learned from real Pew surveys. Here is the evidence — and exactly how close it lands.

Calibration

Calibrated, not asserted.

Every Plebify response includes a per-topic-class calibration confidence band derived from 42 published Pew surveys. We tell you when the swarm is trustworthy and when it isn't — topic-by-topic.

Plebify is augmented research, not poll replacement. Use it to pre-test wording, iterate on segments, and generate hypothesis-quality numbers fast. For high-stakes claims, validate against a real survey.
