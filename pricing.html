FAQ

Common questions

Is this a replacement for a real poll?

No — it is augmented research. Use Plebify for fast directional reads, message pre-testing, and iterating across many variants. For high-stakes or publishable claims, validate against a real survey.

What counts as a "report"?

One question run against the swarm. You get the headline distribution, a party crosstab (Dem / Rep / Ind), representative voices, a calibration-confidence band, and a downloadable PDF + JSON.

How accurate is it?

Calibrated against the Pew American Trends Panel, topline error runs about 0.10 TVD on binary opinion and ~0.15 on divided-opinion questions. Every report reports its own expected-accuracy band by scale class.

What is under the hood?

A census-mixed persona swarm answering as verbalized probabilities (Llama 3.3 70B), linear-pooled and corrected by an isotonic calibration fit on real Pew surveys. Larger libraries and custom audiences are available on higher tiers.
